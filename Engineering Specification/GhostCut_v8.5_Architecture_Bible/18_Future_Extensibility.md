# 18 — Future Extensibility

New analyzers register as read-only runtimes; new repairs register as bounded transactional operators. Each declares artifacts, capabilities, cost, policy schema, evidence fields, compatibility versions, privacy impact, and benchmark fixtures. They may not bypass source immutability, protected masks, rollback, or benchmark gates.

Reflection, shadow, smoke, water, depth, and model-specific modules are deferred until held-out failures show that existing primitives cannot solve a material gap. Version Runtime API, artifact schema, policy schema, explainability schema, and benchmarks independently; migrations must be explicit, tested, and reversible.

Release checklist: registry compatibility; fixture/benchmark additions; telemetry; accessible UI explanations; no critical regression; documented fallback/rollback. This keeps GhostCut extensible without repeating monolithic pipeline growth.
